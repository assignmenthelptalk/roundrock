### What Is a Salt-Free Water Softener System?

A salt-free water softener system is a [conditioner](/water-conditioner-installation/) that changes how hardness minerals behave instead of removing them. Calcium and magnesium stay in the water, but they are less likely to form hard scale. The unit installs on the main line, needs no electricity or a drain in most designs and has no regeneration cycle. Because it adds no sodium, it suits homeowners watching salt intake.

Round Rock water is hard, and the City of Round Rock draws surface water from Lake Georgetown and Lake Travis and adds Edwards Aquifer groundwater. The City's 2025 water quality report put average hardness at 249 mg/L, about 14.6 grains per gallon, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard. A salt-free system reduces scale in that water but does not give the slick, mineral-free feel of a [salt-based](/water-softener-guides/salt-vs-salt-free-water-softener/) softener. ## Which Salt-Free Water Conditioners Are Available in Round Rock, TX?

Three salt-free technologies are installed for scale: template assisted crystallization (TAC) systems, electronic descalers and magnetic water conditioners.

### Template Assisted Crystallization (TAC) Systems

A template assisted crystallization (TAC) system passes water over specially made media that turns dissolved hardness minerals into microscopic crystals. Those crystals stay suspended and resist attaching to pipes, heaters and fixtures. TAC is the most widely used salt-free method, and the media is replaced on a schedule that depends on water use. Manufacturers commonly rate the media at about 5 to 8 years under normal conditions.

### Electronic Descaler Installation

An electronic descaler wraps a coil around the main pipe and sends a changing electronic signal through the water. It installs in under an hour without cutting pipe. Independent evidence of how well electronic descalers prevent scale is mixed, and results vary with water chemistry, so we explain what to expect before you buy.

### Magnetic Water Conditioner Installation

A magnetic water conditioner clamps magnets onto the pipe to alter how minerals form scale. It is the lowest-cost, lowest-maintenance option. Independent evidence for magnetic conditioners is limited, and we recommend them only where a light scale reduction is the goal.

### Salt-Free vs. Salt-Based: Which Is Right for Round Rock Water?

A salt-based softener removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange, and it delivers true soft water. A salt-free system reduces scale but leaves the minerals in place.

- **Choose salt-based** if your water is very hard, you want soft-feeling water, or spotting on glass and fixtures bothers you most.
- **Choose salt-free** if you want no salt, no drain connection and minimal upkeep, and scale protection is the main goal.

> **Not a good fit if...** your water carries iron or sulfur (these need filtration first) or you want fully softened water for skin, hair and laundry. A free test at your tap decides it.
