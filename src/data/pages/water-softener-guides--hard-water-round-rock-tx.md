## What Do Round Rock Homeowners Need to Know About Hard Water?

Three questions cover the basics: what hard water is, why Round Rock water is hard and how hard it measures.

<div class="svc-card">

### What Is Hard Water?

Hard water is water with a high concentration of dissolved calcium and magnesium. These minerals dissolve as water moves through rock and soil, and they stay in the water until something removes them. Hardness is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate or in grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The U.S. Geological Survey rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 as moderately hard, 121 to 180 as hard and over 180 as very hard.

</div>

### Why Is Round Rock Water Hard?

Round Rock water is hard because the lakes and aquifer it draws from sit in limestone country. According to the City of Round Rock's 2025 water quality report, the supply is surface water primarily from Lake Georgetown, groundwater from the Edwards Aquifer and surface water from Lake Travis treated by the Brushy Creek Regional Utility Authority, and those sources contain dissolved limestone and other mineral-bearing rock formations that add calcium and magnesium. According to a 1987 U.S. Geological Survey report by M. E. Dorsey and Diana L. Slagle titled “Hydrologic and Geologic Data for the Edwards Aquifer Recharge Zone near Georgetown, Williamson County, Texas, 1986-87,” the Edwards aquifer in that area includes the Edwards Limestone, the overlying Georgetown Limestone and the underlying Comanche Peak Limestone, and dissolved solids ranged from 320 to 1,300 milligrams per liter in samples from selected wells and from 251 to 290 milligrams per liter in samples from selected streams.

</div>

### How Hard Is Round Rock Water?

The City of Round Rock's 2025 water quality report put average hardness at 249 mg/L, about 14.6 grains per gallon, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard. The report's results table lists hardness from 100 to 358 ppm, about 5.8 to 20.9 grains per gallon, with an average of 258 ppm, about 15.1 grains per gallon, and calcium from 45 to 95 ppm. Readings vary because the mix of lake water and aquifer groundwater changes, so the number at your tap can differ. A free test at your address gives the figure that matters for choosing equipment.

### What Hard Water Does to a Home

- **Appliances:** Scale builds inside water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines. A heater coated in scale works harder to heat the same water.
- **Pipes and fixtures:** Mineral deposits narrow pipes, clog showerheads and leave white crust on faucets.
- **Skin and hair:** Hard water reacts with soap to form a film that leaves skin dry and hair dull.
- **Laundry:** Minerals stiffen fabric, dull colors and reduce how well detergent cleans.
- **Dishes and glass:** Spots and cloudy film appear after the dishwasher runs.

Hard water is a quality and comfort problem. The City of Round Rock's reports list results for regulated contaminants separately, and hardness is not a health-based limit.

### Solutions for Hard Water

Three options reduce the effects of hard water:

1. **A water softener** removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange and gives true soft water. [Water Softener Installation in Round Rock, TX](/water-softener-installation/) is the most complete fix for the whole house.
2. **A conditioner or descaler** changes how minerals form scale without removing them. It reduces scale with less maintenance but does not make water feel soft.
3. **Point-of-use treatment**, such as a shower filter or an under-sink system, improves one tap and leaves the rest of the house untreated.

Most Round Rock homeowners choose a [salt-based](/water-softener-guides/salt-vs-salt-free-water-softener/) softener when hardness is the main complaint. A test first confirms hardness and checks for iron, which affects [sizing](/water-softener-guides/water-softener-size-guide/).

<summary>Does a water softener remove minerals I need?</summary>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Why does my glassware spot even with a rinse aid?</summary>

Dissolved minerals dry on glass as white spots. Softened water stops the spotting at the source.

<summary>Can hard water damage my water heater?</summary>

Scale insulates the heating element or tank bottom, which makes the heater work harder and shortens its life. Flushing helps, and softened water prevents most of the buildup.
