### How to Choose a Water Softener System

If your water tests above 10.5 grains per gallon and you want the hardness minerals removed, a salt-based softener is the system that does it. If scale control matters more than full softening, or salt and a drain line are a problem, a salt-free system reduces scale instead.

| System | What it does | Needs salt | Needs a drain | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salt-based softener | Removes calcium and magnesium by ion exchange | Yes | Yes | Hardness above 10.5 gpg, soft-water feel for skin and laundry |
| Dual tank softener | Two tanks alternate, so soft water never pauses | Yes | Yes | Large households, heavy water use, businesses |
| Salt-free conditioner | Changes how scale forms so it does not stick | No | No | Low maintenance, scale protection |

A household of four using 320 gallons a day at 14.6 grains per gallon removes about 4,700 grains daily, so a 48,000-grain unit regenerates about every 10 days. A free hardness test at your tap replaces that example with your own number.

> **Not a good fit if...** you are on a private well with iron or sulfur. Those need filtration before a softener, and a test shows which. See [Water Treatment Services in Round Rock, TX](/water-treatment-and-testing/) for well and filtration options.

<summary>What is the best water softener system for Round Rock homes?</summary>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can I install a water softener myself in Texas?</summary>

Many homeowners install their own softener, but licensing and permit rules for water treatment and plumbing work are set by the state and the city, so check the Texas requirements for your job before you start. Anyone who installs a softener for a fee should hold the licence the state requires, so ask for the licence number and proof of insurance before the work starts.

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What is the difference between a water softener and a water conditioner?</summary>

A softener removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange, and a conditioner changes how those minerals behave so scale does not stick. The softener lowers the hardness number, and the conditioner leaves it where it is.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does a water softener use electricity?</summary>

A softener with an electronic control head uses a small amount of power to run its regeneration schedule, and it needs a nearby outlet. A salt-free conditioner needs no power.
