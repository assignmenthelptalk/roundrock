## Why Teravista Residents Need a Water Softener

A utility district, not the City, bills most Teravista homes, but the water comes from the City of Round Rock system. Community Impact reported in January 2024 that TeraVista Williamson County MUD No. 10 and No. 11 are two of five districts that buy wholesale water and wastewater service from the City, and the City's June 2022 watering notice applies its Stage 1 schedule to residents of districts that are wholesale customers. The City's 2025 water quality report puts that supply at 249 mg/L average hardness, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard.

At 14.6 gpg, a household of four using 320 gallons a day removes about 4,700 grains daily, so a 48,000-grain salt-based softener regenerates about every 10 days. A free test at your tap gives the reading for your home, because the City's report is citywide and gives no Teravista figure.

## About Teravista Water Quality

The City of Round Rock supplies surface water from Lake Georgetown and Lake Travis plus Edwards Aquifer groundwater, and it disinfects with chloramine. Its 2025 report gave an average chloramine residual of 2.15 ppm, an average pH of 7.53 and an average calcium level of 70 ppm, and its results table lists hardness from 100 to 358 ppm. Your district bill names your provider, and a test at your tap shows what reaches your home.
